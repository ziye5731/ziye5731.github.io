## As an Oboist

I started playing the oboe in 2021 at the age of 19.
During my time at Nankai University, I have performed a variety of orchestral works and chamber music pieces.

Below are some selected concerts and works I have performed:

- Bizet: *Carmen Suite No. 1* (June 2026, second oboist and English hornist)
- Bruch: *Violin Concerto No. 1, Op. 26* (June 2026, second oboist)
- [J.S. Bach: *Concerto for Violin and Oboe in D Minor, BWV 1060R*](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV17RZ9BtEDy/?spm_id_from=333.1387.upload.video_card.click&vd_source=c40a8876b198ff0ce0a517c69971d71e) (December 2025, solo oboist)
- Beethoven: *Symphony No. 1, Op. 21* (June 2024, principal oboist)


## OpenReed
OpenReed is an open-source project that focuses on 3D-printed accessories/tools/machines for double-reed instruments, which aims to decrease the cost of playing the oboe and the bassoon, and promote innovations in reed studies.

Links: [Project page](https://openreed.github.io), [GitHub](https://github.com/openreed).
